Privacy policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026 · Publisher: Olivier Popiers · Contact: oliviertheandroidmaker@gmail.com

Your data stays on your phone

“Sans…” has no account and no server. Counters, dates and relapse history are stored only in the app’s private storage on your device. We cannot access them.

Backup

The “Back up” feature writes your counters to a file, at the location you choose (phone, Drive…). The app never sends this file anywhere; its storage is up to the service you picked.

Advertising

The app is free and shows one banner ad served by Google AdMob. To display it, Google and its partners may collect and process data on your device (advertising ID, IP address, technical device information, ad interactions) to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. None of your counter data is transmitted.

Consent

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, a consent message (Google User Messaging Platform) is shown on first launch. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy choices. Without consent, no personalized ads are requested.

Notifications

Milestone notifications are generated locally on your phone; no data is sent. You can turn them off in the app or system settings.

Data sharing

We do not sell or share your data, other than the exchanges with Google AdMob described above. More: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Deletion

Uninstalling the app deletes all its local data. You can reset your advertising ID in Android settings.

Your rights (GDPR)

For any question or request about your data, write to oliviertheandroidmaker@gmail.com.
